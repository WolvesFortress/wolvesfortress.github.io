The World of Luprune

Nine elements. Countless secrets.
One very accident-prone lab.

Magic here isn't sparkles and mystery — it's science, and it's everywhere. Welcome to Luprune.

A World Built on Elements

Elemental crystals light the streets, power the forges, and keep the fish cold at the market. Scholars study them obsessively. Nations are built around them. And somewhere, tucked in ancient ruins and half-forgotten caves, are rune artifacts that predate all of it — that nobody quite understands yet.

It's a wonderful, weird, and occasionally explosive place to live.

Petalcove

A Quiet Town That Doesn't Know It's About to Be Important

Tucked between a whispering forest and a lily-covered pond, Petalcove smells like flowers and woodsmoke. There's a flower shop, a tavern, a blacksmith, a sawmill, and a lab with scorch marks on every wall.

It's peaceful. Nothing ever really happens in Petalcove.
