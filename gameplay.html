How to Play

Explore. Fight.
Get grabbed by vines. Figure it out.

Luprune is the kind of game that trusts you to work things out. There's a lot to work out.

Exploration

A World Worth Getting Lost In

No quest markers. No minimap. Just a world full of people with opinions, a journal that writes itself as you talk to them, and something interesting hidden around every corner.

Crystal caves. Ancient ruins. Hidden springs that nobody's found in centuries. The best discoveries in Luprune are always a little further than you planned to walk.

🗺️ [Exploration screenshot — open landscape, forest path, distant fortress]
Combat

Nine Elements. Infinite Combinations.

Soak an enemy in water. Strike with lightning. Watch what happens.

Every element interacts with every other — in combat, in the environment, and in the moments where you accidentally freeze the floor and slide directly into a boss. Mastering elemental combinations is less a mechanic and more a lifestyle.

⚡ [Combat screenshot — elemental effects, dynamic action]

The Nine Elements

"Every crystal hums at its own frequency. What happens when two meet? ...It depends on the crystals."

🔥 Pyro Things that shouldn't be left unattended
💧 Aqua Life, flow, and very wet enemies
🌿 Natura Growth, healing, suspiciously sentient vines
🌬️ Aero Wind, sound, dramatic entrances
🏔️ Terra Strength and an unreasonable amount of rock
❄️ Cryo Ice, cold, and refrigeration that actually works
⚡ Electro Please don't mix this with Aqua
☀️ Lumina So nobody walks into walls at night
🌑 Umbra Darkness, silence, probably fine
Dungeons

Part Puzzle. Part Gauntlet.
Part "Why is the Floor Moving."

Every dungeon in Luprune has its own logic, its own traps, and its own boss that has absolutely no interest in making things easy for you.

They hit hard. They have phases. And they will not announce themselves with glowing arrows. You'll learn by watching. You'll win by adapting.
